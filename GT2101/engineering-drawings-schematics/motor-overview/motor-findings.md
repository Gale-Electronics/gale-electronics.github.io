# Retired — see the live page

This file used to hold a **saved copy of a rendered web page**: the 22 August 2026 version of
*GT2101 — Motor Findings*, complete with its breadcrumb, `meta-og:` tags and site footer. It
was not a source document and it was not the original of anything.

**The canonical page is [`/GT2101/technical-notes/motor-findings/`](/GT2101/technical-notes/motor-findings/)**
(`technical-notes/motor-findings/index.md` in this repository). It is newer, and everything
the snapshot held has been checked into it.

## Why it was retired — 5 September 2026

Kept alongside the drawings it was a live hazard, in three ways:

1. **It used a third confidence scheme.** `[CONFIRMED]` / `[REPORTED]` / `[UNCONFIRMED]`,
   against the ✅ / 📄 / ❓ used everywhere else in this archive and the
   `[HW]` / `[1st]` / `[2nd]` / `[SITE]` scheme in `folder-findings.md`. Three schemes for one
   idea is how a marker gets dropped in translation.
2. **It over-marked.** It gave the motor's drive voltages and its tacho figure as
   `[CONFIRMED]` when neither has been measured on a running deck — both are 📄 on the live
   page, which is correct. It also stated the 6-pin DIN pin allocation as confirmed, where the
   live page correctly records the connector accounting as unresolved.
3. **It was stale.** It predates the corrections of 4 and 5 September 2026, including the
   LM324/LM339 role swap on the motor PCB.

## What was rescued before it went

The two NMB example part numbers — `M1N6FB08C` and `M1N10FB08G` — which the live page had lost
and which are the only evidence behind the `M1N` naming-pattern lead. They were recovered on
4 September 2026 and are on the live page now. A later sentence-by-sentence comparison, on
5 September 2026, found **nothing else** in the snapshot that the live page lacked.

⚠ **The lesson, which is the reason this stub exists rather than a silent deletion:** the file
looked like a duplicate from a distance and held one unique thing. *Grep for a document's
unique strings before deleting it.* That has now caught something four times in this archive.
