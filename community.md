# Follow & contribute

The Gale Electronics Archive brings together the history, people, design and engineering behind the GS401 loudspeakers and GT2101 turntable. Use this website for the original sources, follow new discoveries on social media, and join other owners for practical discussion.

## Find the right place

### The historical archive

[Explore the Gale Electronics Archive](/). Original photographs, brochures, adverts, engineering drawings, interviews and research notes live here, with sources and uncertainties recorded alongside them.

### Facebook: history and discoveries

[Follow Gale Electronics and Design on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61574017514396) for period advertising, archive photographs, stories about the people behind Gale and updates to the collection.

### Facebook group: owners and restoration

[Join Gale Electronics Owners & Enthusiasts](https://www.facebook.com/groups/3977181785904204/) for restoration questions, practical advice, listening experiences and discussions with other owners.

### TikTok: short archive stories

[Watch @galegt2101 on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@galegt2101) for short videos and photo stories about the GT2101, GS401A, original advertising and the designers and engineers behind the equipment.

<h2 id="contribute">Contribute to the archive</h2>

Do you have an original brochure, advert, photograph, letter or memory of working with Gale? [Email vintagegaleuk@gmail.com](mailto:vintagegaleuk@gmail.com?subject=Gale%20archive%20contribution).

If you can, include:

- What the item shows and its approximate date.
- Who created it, where it came from and any original publication details.
- The names of people pictured, where known.
- How you would like the contribution credited.
- Whether you own the material or know who does, and whether we may publish it in the archive and on its social channels.

Original scans and photographs at their best available resolution are especially useful. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information. We can discuss redaction before publishing an item.

First-hand recollections are welcome even when dates are uncertain. We distinguish personal memories from contemporary documents and keep open questions visible.

### Corrections and credits

If you can identify an image, correct a caption or supply a missing credit, please email us with the relevant page link. You can also [open an issue in the archive’s GitHub repository](https://github.com/Gale-Electronics/gale-electronics.github.io/issues).

Copyrights remain with their original creators unless an item states otherwise. Please check individual credits before reusing material.
