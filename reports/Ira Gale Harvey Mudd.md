# Trace Ira Gale’s Harvey Mudd Years

The available evidence adds a specific account of Ira Dennis Gale’s time at Harvey Mudd College, but it does not yet independently verify his enrollment or degree. Jon Mayberry’s archived biography says Gale studied mathematics at Harvey Mudd and graduated in June 1964; it also describes concurrent music study at Scripps College and participation in the Pomona College orchestra. Harvey Mudd’s own history confirms the College was active in the period, but does not name Gale. The article also names Dr. Sao Win as a classmate, though that connection likewise rests on Mayberry’s account. The most useful next step is to locate the cited 1964 Spectrum yearbook page or request verification through Harvey Mudd’s Registrar.

## Mayberry’s biography reports a 1964 mathematics degree

The archived article identifies Gale as a mathematics major at Harvey Mudd College in Claremont and says he graduated in June 1964. It points to an image described as his Harvey Mudd “Spectrum” yearbook page, making the yearbook the strongest potential contemporaneous corroboration surfaced by this search. The image itself and an official commencement record were not independently accessible for verification, so the degree, major, and date should currently be attributed to Mayberry rather than stated as independently confirmed facts. ([Mayberry’s archived biography](https://web.archive.org/web/20190920014655/http://0339436.netsolhost.com/WordPress/ira-gale-story/); [yearbook image linked from the article](https://web.archive.org/web/20190920014655/http://0339436.netsolhost.com/WordPress/wp-content/uploads/2011/09/IRA-GALE-HMC-SPECTRUM-2.jpg))

## The College’s timeline confirms context, not attendance

Harvey Mudd’s official timeline records its first four-year graduating class in 1961 and a 1964 Engineering Clinic project. This establishes that the College was operating during the period Mayberry gives for Gale’s studies, but it does not confirm that Gale enrolled, graduated, or took part in any particular program. No searchable official commencement list, catalog entry, or independently accessible 1964 Spectrum page naming Gale was found in the research notes. ([Harvey Mudd College history timeline](https://www.hmc.edu/about/timeline/))

## Music and a named classmate remain single-source claims

Mayberry’s article says Gale spent about half his time studying music at nearby Scripps College and played in the Pomona College orchestra. It also identifies Dr. Sao Win as one of Gale’s Harvey Mudd classmates and connects Win to later high-end audio work. These details could help frame a biographical connection between mathematics, music, and Gale’s later audio career, but the notes found no independent college source or testimony confirming them. They should remain explicitly attributed to Mayberry unless corroborating records emerge. ([Mayberry’s archived biography](https://web.archive.org/web/20190920014655/http://0339436.netsolhost.com/WordPress/ira-gale-story/))

## Yearbook and Registrar records are the best follow-ups

The linked Spectrum page is the most direct lead: retrieving the original yearbook could verify Gale’s listing, class year, and any printed activities, while avoiding assumptions based only on the article’s caption. Harvey Mudd’s Registrar is the official route for academic-record and degree-verification inquiries; its page explains record access and privacy constraints. A request should ask what historical enrollment or graduation verification can be released, rather than assume that a transcript or student record is public. Further confirmation of the Scripps and Pomona claims would require those institutions’ contemporaneous records or independent testimony. ([Harvey Mudd Registrar](https://www.harveymudd.org/registrar/); [yearbook image linked from the article](https://web.archive.org/web/20190920014655/http://0339436.netsolhost.com/WordPress/wp-content/uploads/2011/09/IRA-GALE-HMC-SPECTRUM-2.jpg))

## Conclusion

The research yields a credible, specific biographical lead: Mayberry places Gale at Harvey Mudd as a mathematics student who graduated in 1964 and links him to nearby music study and a fellow student who later worked in audio. At present, those personal details remain documented by the archived biography, while Harvey Mudd’s public history supplies context only. Finding the yearbook page or receiving Registrar confirmation would materially strengthen the account.
