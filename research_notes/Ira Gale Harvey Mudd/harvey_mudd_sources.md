# Ira Gale at Harvey Mudd

## What can be established about his attendance and studies?

### Takeaway
The strongest accessible evidence is Jon Mayberry’s archived GaleAudio biography, which states that Ira Dennis Gale majored in mathematics at Harvey Mudd and graduated in June 1964. The article also says he played in the Pomona College orchestra and studied music at Scripps, but I found no independent public college record confirming those details.

### Cited Findings
- The 2019 archive snapshot of Jon Mayberry’s article says Gale majored in mathematics at Harvey Mudd College in Claremont and graduated in June 1964; it also says he was a member of the Pomona College orchestra and spent about half his time at nearby Scripps College studying music. The article links an image identified as Ira Gale’s Harvey Mudd “Spectrum” yearbook page. [Archived biography](https://web.archive.org/web/20190920014655/http://0339436.netsolhost.com/WordPress/ira-gale-story/); [yearbook image referenced by the article](https://web.archive.org/web/20190920014655/http://0339436.netsolhost.com/WordPress/wp-content/uploads/2011/09/IRA-GALE-HMC-SPECTRUM-2.jpg)
- Harvey Mudd’s official history says its first four-year class graduated in 1961 and records the College’s 1964 Engineering Clinic project. This confirms the College was operating in the period of Gale’s reported attendance, but does not independently verify his enrollment or degree. [Harvey Mudd College history timeline](https://www.hmc.edu/about/timeline/)

### Inferences
- The article’s specific identification of the yearbook image as Gale’s HMC Spectrum page supports its account that Gale attended Harvey Mudd; the underlying yearbook page itself was not independently retrievable through the research browser during this search.

### Gaps
- No searchable official Harvey Mudd commencement list, archived catalog entry, transcript, or independently accessible 1964 Spectrum page confirming Gale’s degree, major, dates, or student activities was located.
- The claim that he studied music at Scripps and played in Pomona’s orchestra remains sourced only to Mayberry’s biography in the archived page.

## What additional Harvey Mudd connections or activities are documented?

### Takeaway
Mayberry’s article connects Gale’s mathematical studies with music and names Dr. Sao Win as a Harvey Mudd classmate who later worked in high-end audio. Search results did not produce a reliable independent source proving the classmate relationship, and no further HMC-specific projects, honors, clubs, or faculty connections were found.

### Cited Findings
- Mayberry’s archived article states that Dr. Sao Win was one of Gale’s Harvey Mudd classmates and describes Win’s later turntable and phono-cartridge work. [Archived biography](https://web.archive.org/web/20190920014655/http://0339436.netsolhost.com/WordPress/ira-gale-story/)
- Harvey Mudd’s current registrar page explains that the College maintains academic records and that student information release is governed by FERPA; it gives an official contact route for degree-verification inquiries. [Harvey Mudd Registrar](https://www.harveymudd.org/registrar/)

### Inferences
- The convergence of mathematics, music, and later audio engineering makes a useful biographical thread, but the relationship should be presented as Mayberry’s account unless a yearbook, college record, or independent testimony is obtained.

### Gaps
- No primary Harvey Mudd source naming Gale or Sao Win together was located.
- The archive’s embedded yearbook and other image assets could not be opened with the web research tool, so their printed captions and surrounding page context remain unverified here.
